Reservation Terms

Draft for launch preparation — obtain local legal review before accepting payments.

A Founding 50 reservation is intended to reserve priority for a product that is still under development. It is not a final retail purchase and does not guarantee a fixed shipment date, final material, color, geometry, compatibility, or final retail price.

The reservation amount should be refundable until the customer affirmatively converts it into a product order under final purchase terms. The reservation should be credited toward the final purchase if the customer proceeds.

Before public launch, replace this draft with terms that identify the legal seller, contact information, refund procedure, currency, applicable taxes, dispute process, and any jurisdiction-specific consumer rights.
